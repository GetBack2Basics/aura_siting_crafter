📌 1. Purpose of Planning Submission

The Macquarie Coal Complex represents one of the most strategically compelling industrial transition opportunities in regional NSW. With direct access to high-capacity 330kV transmission infrastructure, regional rail, arterial transport routes, and Hunter Water recycled effluent networks, the precinct is an ideal candidate for advanced manufacturing, clean energy industries, and sovereign AI compute infrastructure.

This submission provides planning officers with evidence-based recommendations to ensure that the Finalisation Report, State Significant Rezoning instrument, and Precinct DCP provide commercial development certainty while safeguarding surrounding communities (Teralba, Barnsley, Awaba) and regional environmental catchments.

🗺️ 2. Key Planning Considerations & Land Budget

A. Realizing True Net Developable Area (NDA) vs. Gross Footprint

While initial masterplans present gross precinct figures (~45 ha), statutory constraints on post-mining terrain reduce buildable pad space: -3.8 ha for 30m riparian creek corridors, -2.1 ha for utility easements, -4.6 ha for slopes >5% requiring major civil works, and -15.2 ha for the declared Tailings Storage Facility (TSF) safety zone. The true Net Developable Area is 15.2 ha (immediate) to 28.5 ha (post-remediation). Statutory zoning maps should explicitly delineate these buildable pads.

B. Proactive Acoustic Protection for Adjacent Communities

Under the NSW EPA Noise Policy for Industry (2017) and AS 1055:2018, 24/7 industrial activities (such as cooling plant and backup power) must protect night-time residential amenity in Teralba, Barnsley, and Awaba. A 500m acoustic compliance setback combined with mandatory perimeter acoustic bunding and low-noise mechanical equipment should be embedded in the Precinct DCP.

C. Water Stewardship: Mandating 100% Recycled Water Cooling

High-tech computing and heavy industry require significant thermal cooling. The Macquarie Precinct is well-situated to connect to Hunter Water's recycled wastewater networks. Mandating 100% closed-loop liquid cooling or tertiary-treated recycled water preserves potable drinking water supplies across the Lake Macquarie catchment.

D. Multi-Hazard Resilience & Climate Adaptation

The precinct complies fully with national statutory standards: AS/NZS 1170.2 (Region A4 wind engineering), AS 1170.4 (Seismic Hazard Ground Motion), complete exclusion from 1% AEP floodways (NCC 2022 Part B1), and geotechnical zoning compliant with Australian Geomechanics Society (AGS 2007) landslide guidelines.

E. Clean Energy Circularity & Pumped Hydro Storage

The natural ~120m hydraulic head between the upper ridge and the existing pit void offers up to 49.0 MWh of potential pumped hydro energy storage, providing on-site 24/7 renewable firming directly aligned with the Federal Net Zero Economy Authority's objectives.

⚖️ 3. Five Specific Planning Recommendations

# Planning Domain Recommended Statutory Mechanism Intended Planning Outcome
1 Net Developable Area DCP Structure Plan Map Delineate clear Net Developable Pad Boundaries (15.2–28.5 ha) separate from riparian corridors, steep slopes (>5%), and TSF buffers to provide commercial certainty for incoming proponents.
2 Acoustic Protection Precinct DCP Clause Establish a mandatory 500m acoustic compliance buffer from heavy mechanical cooling plant to sensitive residential receivers (Teralba, Barnsley, Awaba) under the NSW EPA Noise Policy for Industry (2017).
3 Water Preservation Rezoning Instrument Clause Insert a requirement that any high-demand thermal cooling use 100% closed-loop or non-potable recycled water from regional WWTWs, strictly prohibiting raw potable water extraction.
4 Energy Circularity Special Infrastructure Corridor Designate the pit void and hydraulic easement as a Protected Clean Energy & Storage Corridor to enable future pumped hydro or battery storage integration.
5 Digital Open Access NSW Spatial Digital Twin Integrate all final precinct masterplan layers and environmental constraint vectors directly into the NSW Spatial Digital Twin and SEED portals in open GDA2020 format.
