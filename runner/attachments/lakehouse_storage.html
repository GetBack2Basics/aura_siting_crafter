Lakehouse Storage & Table Directory Structure (v2)

All spatial tables are cataloged under org_catalog.fgsdb.* on Wherobots Cloud and persisted directly in cloud object storage at s3://wherobots-user-storage/aura_siting_v2/ strictly adhering to GDA2020 geographic standard EPSG:7844.

s3://wherobots-user-storage/aura_siting_v2/
├── national_sensitive_receptors/ [ACARA, NHSD & OSM National POIs (EPSG:7844)]
├── national_electricity_grid/ [GA, AEMO, Powerlink, VicGrid, Western Power (EPSG:7844)]
├── national_multi_hazards/ [Landslide, NSHA Earthquake, TCHA Cyclone, Inundation (EPSG:7844)]
├── national_cadastre_gnaf/ [15.4M Geoscape & State Lot/Plans (EPSG:7844)]
├── abs_demographics_meshblocks/ [1.18M Meshblocks Partitioned (EPSG:7844)]
├── state_biodiversity_constraints/ [NSW Bionet, QLD VMA, VIC NVIM, WA DBCA (EPSG:7844)]
├── precinct_net_developable_v2/ [Buildable Pad Space (EPSG:7844)]
└── exports/datacenter_candidates_v2.parquet [DuckDB-WASM Range Query Cache]
