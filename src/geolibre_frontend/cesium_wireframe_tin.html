💡 ELI10: What is a 3D DEM Wireframe (TIN)?

Imagine draping a glowing cyan spiderweb over real mountains and valleys!

  • TIN (Triangles): We measure the exact ground height (from 20m in the creek to 138m on the ridge) and draw triangles connecting every spot.
  • Terrain Exaggeration (1.5x): Stretches the hills slightly so you can easily spot slopes and flat areas.
  • Pitch & Bearing Sliders: Tilt and spin the 3D globe like an engineering video game!
