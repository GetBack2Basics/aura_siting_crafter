1. Executive Summary & Statutory Siting Merits

The Macquarie Coal Complex Transformation Precinct represents one of the most strategically significant post-mining brownfield transition precincts in Australia. Located within the City of Lake Macquarie with direct frontage to Transgrid 330kV transmission lines and the Main Northern Railway, the precinct is primed for sovereign digital compute, clean energy firming, and advanced manufacturing.

This site-specific report supplements the National Siting Baseline by detailing localized geotechnical certifications, 3-phase developable pad geometry, acoustic bund buffering, and closed-loop non-potable cooling integration.
